0:00 · 20sHook — the frame

Say"Frontier models are powerful — but expensive, and flat-expensive forever: the ten-thousandth call costs the same as the first. So we asked: can a cheap model, wrapped in the right harness, reach frontier quality? Same gpt-4.1-mini on both sides of this screen — the right one has our harness. Watch what the harness adds."

0:20 · 20sThe bet

Say"Don't reach for a bigger model — build a harness around a cheap one. One reward gates the agent; it samples and self-verifies within a run, and it remembers every fixed mistake run-to-run. No GPU, no fine-tuning, on Azure Foundry — exactly the cost-optimized constraint."

3:10 · 70sDemo — the money slide: cost via escalation

DoScroll to the Cost thesis panel → click Measure cost (takes ~1–2 min — talk over it, or pre-run once).

Say"Same hard reasoning problems. Always paying gpt-5.1 solves 10 of 15, at full price. Cheap-first plus escalate — mini+harness, and the reward escalates to gpt-5.1 only on the ones it can't crack — hits the same 10 of 15 at about 72% of the cost, paying the premium on just 6 tasks. Frontier quality — you pay the frontier price only where you must."

4:20 · 20sWhy it matters — Omnia
